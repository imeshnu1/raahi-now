The Plan

One domain: now.raahi.io

Raahi NOW lives on a subdomain of raahi.io, the domain Raahi already owns. No new domain, one brand, one login, one wallet. raahi.io stays the main home; now.raahi.io is where live Drops, Asks, unlocks and local storefronts happen.

Anti-viral by design

  • No followers, no like counts, no save counts, no leaderboard, no endless scroll.
  • Drops expire at the end of their window. Yesterday's tip is dead.
  • When a Drop hits its daily unlock cap, it pauses for the day. The place protects itself.
  • Every recommendation comes from a named, verified local. No AI recommendations, no anonymous tips.
  • We do not sell people. We give people a storefront.

The money, honestly

Unlocks run $1–$3 equivalent, an Ask costs $5 equivalent. Locals keep 80%, Raahi keeps 20%. Locals choose their own payout currency. Payments are currency-agnostic: Ninjji (native, preferred, lowest fees), other crypto, card, and fiat USD / JPY / LKR to start. In this demo, no real money moves.

How it expands

Markets are configuration, not code: country, then city or region, then local, then Drop. NYC, Boston and Ellsworth/Acadia (USA) launch first. Japan (Kyoto) and Sri Lanka (Sigiriya) are built into the data model now and open for paid launch after each country's laws, payment and partner pass. A sixth market later is setup, not a rebuild.

Social is a billboard

Every Drop can be exported as a Raahi-branded card to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reddit and Facebook. You can watch it anywhere. You can only unlock the exact location, and book the person, on Raahi.
