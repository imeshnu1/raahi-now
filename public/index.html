One home: now.raahi.io

Raahi NOW lives here, on a corner of raahi.io — the home Raahi already has. One brand, one login, one wallet. raahi.io is the main home. This is where you'll find live Drops, ask Izzy, and book locals.

Made to stay small and honest

  • No followers. No like counts. No endless scroll. Just people and places.
  • Drops don't last. When a Drop's time is up, it's gone — yesterday's tip stays yesterday's.
  • Each Drop can only be unlocked a few times a day. When it reaches its limit, it rests — so the place never gets overrun.
  • Every tip comes from a real local, by name, checked by us. Nothing anonymous, and nothing written by AI.
  • We do not sell people. We give people a storefront.

The money, honestly

Unlocking a Drop costs about $1–$3. Asking Izzy costs $5. Locals keep 80% of it, Raahi keeps 20%. Locals choose how they get paid. You can pay with Ninjji (our home currency, with the lowest fees), other crypto, a card, or local money — USD, JPY, or LKR to start. For now this is a preview, so no real money moves.

Where we're growing

We grow one place at a time: a country, then a city, then the locals who know it best. New York City, Boston, and Ellsworth/Acadia (USA) come first. Kyoto in Japan and Sigiriya in Sri Lanka are already taking shape — they'll open for paid bookings once we've done right by each country's rules and found the right local partners. Adding the next place is careful work, not a rebuild.
